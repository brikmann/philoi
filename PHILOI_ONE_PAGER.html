Philoi
Lock in with your friends.
A gamified social-accountability app for Wilfrid Laurier + University of Waterloo students — study, gym and deep-work sessions you do together, with streaks, challenges and a flame you grow as a group.
PRE-LAUNCH · WAITLIST OPEN

What it is

🔥 Campfires
Group accountability circles with your friends or your class — show up together.
🔒 Lock-ins
Start a focus / gym / study session; your friends see you put in the work.
⚔ Challenges
Head-to-head and group races to out-work each other over the week.
📈 Streaks · ranks · the flame
Grow and customize your fire, keep your streak, climb the ladder.
