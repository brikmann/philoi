# Philoi — Monetization

## Tiers — Free · Ignite · Blaze
The membership scales with **fire intensity** (a spark grows into a blaze): **Free → Ignite → Blaze.** Ignite and Blaze are the two paid tiers. Upsell copy stays on-theme: *"Light it up," "You're on Fire."*

- **Free** — the entire core loop (lock-ins, campfires, chat, ranks, flame meter, challenges) + a taste of cosmetics. No Forge Pass, minimal/no ember earning.
- **Ignite** (entry paid, ~$3–4/mo) — flame skins + banners + frames, the **Forge Pass (standard track)**, an ember flow, 1080p clips, more routines/campfires, the persistent "on Fire" ember-glow.
- **Blaze** (premium, ~$6–8/mo) — everything in Ignite **plus** the **Forge Pass premium track** (more/better rewards), **Blaze-exclusive** skins & badges, bigger ember rewards, the top video tier (60fps / longer clips), the most of everything. The flex tier.

> **Naming hygiene:** keep lowercase **"your fire"** for the solo campfire (home page 1); tier names are **Ignite / Blaze** (capitalized). ("Philoi Fire" may stay as the umbrella name for the paid membership, or be retired in favor of the tier names — TBD.)

## The one rule (non-negotiable)
**Never sell anything that fakes effort.** No buying XP, rank, streaks, PRs, or leaderboard position. That honest-effort signal is the entire app. Everything paid is **cosmetic** or **convenience** — never function. Core lock-ins, campfires, joining, and chat stay free forever. Monetize the flex, not the function.

---

## Cosmetics — the flex (best category)
- **Flame skins** — recolor your personal living flame (ember-blue, emerald, violet, molten-gold, seasonal). The flame is the app's hero visual → the single most desirable cosmetic. **Constraint:** a skin recolors the palette but must preserve *intensity = activity* (roaring / steady / going-cold / out stays legible).
- **Profile banners** — animated / gradient fire banners behind the rank hex.
- **Avatar frames + rank-hex glow** — decorate the badge; never buy the rank itself.
- **Campfire skins** — owners dress up the group fire; the whole campfire sees it (social proof pulls other members in).
- **Alt app icons** — cheap to produce, classic premium pull.
- **Premium share cards** — fancier rank-up / PR templates for IG stories. Doubles as a growth loop.
- **Fire glow** — subscribers get a subtle persistent ember/glow on their flame, so being on Philoi Fire is *visible* — the name doubles as the cosmetic and quietly sells itself to everyone who sees them.

## Badges & titles — hard line
- **Earned** badges (streaks, PRs, challenges won) stay **free** and remain the real status.
- **Paid** badges are clearly **cosmetic / "supporter" flair** + **custom titles** under your name — never styled to look like earned achievements. A bought badge mistakable for an earned one corrupts the signal.

## Convenience — not pay-to-win
- More saved routines; more campfires / bigger member caps.
- Deeper history & analytics (gym charts, trends).
- **1080p / 60fps video tier** (see spec §23 phase-2 video).
- **Streak insurance** (a couple of "rest day" tokens) — *flag:* give a small amount free too, or it feels like paywalling your streak rather than a nicety.

## Forge Pass — the paid progression track (Ignite + Blaze only)
A **battle-pass-style reward track** riding the XP/rank system you already have. As you earn XP — the same effort that forges your rank — you climb the **Forge Pass** and unlock rewards at milestones: **exclusive badges + embers.** A real reward ladder, not a flat monthly stipend.

- **Paid only.** Free users see it **locked** — a clean upsell: *"the fire you're already earning could be forging rewards."*
- **Rewards = paid-tier-exclusive badges (collectible status symbols) + embers** at XP thresholds. These badges can *only* be earned here.
- **Two tracks:** **Ignite** = the standard track; **Blaze** = a premium track alongside it with more/better rewards at the same milestones (classic two-tier pass).
- **Seasonal** (monthly / per term) — a fresh track + fresh exclusive badges each season, so there's always something to chase and the badges stay rare. Past-season badges are kept (proof you were there early).
- **Name is on-brand:** *Forge Pass* ties to the rank-up **forge** — you forge rewards as you forge your rank.
- *Honesty check (obeys the one rule):* this does **not** sell effort — everyone earns XP the same way; the pass just gives paid users **extra cosmetic rewards for the XP they genuinely earned.** The rank/XP itself is never bought.

## Packaging
- **Bundle into one "Philoi Fire" subscription** — student-friendly (~$3–5/mo). Simple, recurring, easy to explain.
- **Later (phase it): cosmetics shop with a soft currency ("embers")** earned by locking in. Paid users get a monthly ember stipend; free users can grind toward cosmetics (goodwill); spending is tied to actually showing up.
- **Now: Founding members = "Prometheus' Disciple."** A one-time, permanent, **never-offered-again** badge + title for early supporters — the first to carry the fire. Early cash + fierce loyalty from exactly the people you want. (See the lore below.)

## What NOT to sell (write it down so it's never tempting)
XP · rank · streaks-that-look-earned · PRs · leaderboard position · anything that removes accountability. (Forge Pass is fine — it rewards XP you *earned*, it doesn't sell it.)

## Brand lore — Prometheus, Giver of Fire
The founder's mythos and the emotional spine of the brand. **Prometheus stole fire from the gods and gave it to humanity** — and that *is* Philoi: handing people the fire (the drive, the accountability, the community) so they can **rise and ascend.**

- **The founder brands as *Prometheus, Giver of Fire*** — the one who brought the flame.
- **Founding members = *"Prometheus' Disciple"*** — the first to receive the fire and carry it. A permanent, exclusive badge + title; deeply early, deeply loyal.
- The whole app *is* "a chance to rise and ascend" — **forge** up the ranks (Bronze → Infernal), keep **your fire** lit, carry the fire together in **campfires**. Prometheus is the origin story the ranks, the Forge Pass, and the Infernal apex all ladder up from.
- Use it **sparingly and reverently** — in the founding-member offer, onboarding flavor, and the mythos — so it stays powerful, not gimmicky.

## Embers — the currency (earn + buy)
Embers = the **soft cosmetic currency.** Earned by locking in + completing the flame meter + the Forge Pass; **bought** in packs (microtransactions); spent only on cosmetics. Standard coins/gems model (Duolingo gems, etc.) — low-risk, since it buys nothing with power.
- **Remnant of Embers — $4.99** (small)
- **Pile of Embers — $9.99** (medium — flag as best value)
- **Stack of Embers — $15.99** (large)
- **Cosmetics only.** Never buys XP / rank / streaks (the one rule). Show a clear ember balance (top-right, per the flame-meter completion loop).

## Loot boxes — collectible cosmetics (⚠️ PROCEED CAREFULLY)
Randomized boxes bought with **embers**, rewarding **cosmetic badges/skins by rarity**; the Forge Pass can also grant boxes. Rarity ladder (Greek fire-mythology, ascending — consistent with the Prometheus lore):

| Rarity | Box |
|---|---|
| Common | **Kindling Pack** |
| Uncommon | **Ignition Crate** |
| Rare | **The Furnace** |
| Epic | **Vessel of Hestia** *(hearth)* |
| Legendary | **Hephaestus' Chest** *(the forge)* |
| Mythic | **Prometheus' Vault** *(the fire-bringer — apex)* |
| Special / seasonal | **Relic of the Flame** |

**Loot boxes are legally + ethically loaded — do it clean or not at all:**
- **Gambling regulation.** Paid loot boxes (bought with real-money embers) are classified as **gambling** in several countries (Belgium & Netherlands ban them); both app stores require **published drop-odds**. Audience skews students / possibly minors → higher stakes.
- **Brand tension.** Philoi is an *honest-effort, wellbeing* app — a gambling-adjacent mechanic can undercut the trust the brand runs on. Weigh the fit.
- **If you ship it:** cosmetics ONLY (never anything with power); **publish the odds**; a **pity timer** (guaranteed rare after N — no infinite chase); **duplicates convert to embers**; consider **earned-ember-only** boxes to sidestep gambling classification.
- **Safer variants:** every box **guarantees a cosmetic + a chance at a rarer one** (no "you got nothing"), or sell **known bundles** (see exactly what you get) — collectible, zero gambling risk, cleaner brand fit.

## Reward economy — how achievements pay out (phase 2 · obeys Step 18)
> **Build note:** at build time this whole section becomes its own **dedicated spec — `REWARD_ECONOMY.md`** (Inventory data model + reward-scaling engine + grant tables). This doc stays the *why*; the spec is the *how*. Kicked off by CODE_BUILD_PROMPTS Step 21.

The bridge between the **effort economy** (XP / rank / challenges / seasons) and the **shop** (embers / boxes / cosmetics). Achievements pay **rewards scaled to their significance** — bigger/harder/longer/more-selective = rarer payout.

### What can be rewarded
- **Embers** — the flexible currency.
- **Badges** — status/cosmetic, rarity-tiered. **Earned** badges (from real achievements) are FREE, carry **provenance** ("won from X, Season 2"), and **can't be bought** — that exclusivity IS their value. **Paid** badges (Forge Pass / supporter) are clearly separate flair.
- **Loot boxes** — the rarity ladder (Kindling → Prometheus' Vault); granted as rewards *or* bought.
- **XP multipliers** — the placement / season multipliers (that's the *effort* economy, not the shop).

### The Inventory (verifiable)
A server-authoritative **Inventory** screen: **ember balance**, **owned badges** (earned vs bought labelled + provenance), **unopened loot boxes**, **owned cosmetics** (flame skins / banners / frames). "Verifiable" = the server owns the truth, so earned status is real and un-fakeable, and earned-vs-bought is always distinguishable.

### Reward scaling — significance → rarity
Reward rarity/size scales with **difficulty × competition scope × duration × placement.**

| Achievement | Reward |
|---|---|
| Casual H2H win (24h, easy goal) | small embers |
| Multi-day H2H win | embers + **Kindling / Ignition** box |
| **Beat a friend at a ridiculous long goal** (e.g. most lock-in time all semester) | **Epic/Legendary box** (Vessel of Hestia / Hephaestus' Chest) + an **exclusive earned badge** + embers |
| Campfire challenge — completed | completion embers |
| Campfire — top 50% / 25% | embers + **common/uncommon** box |
| **Top 10% in a large campfire** | **Rare/Epic** box (The Furnace / Vessel of Hestia) |
| Campfire top 3 (big fire) | **Epic/Legendary** box + earned badge |
| End-of-season — participated | season **completion badge** + embers |
| End-of-season — top 10% (uni) | **Epic** box + a **seasonal badge** |
| End-of-season — top 10 (uni) | **Legendary** box (Hephaestus' Chest) + rarer seasonal badge |
| **End-of-season — uni #1** | **Mythic** box (Prometheus' Vault) + the **apex seasonal badge** + big embers + the 3× season XP |

### Balance (protect BOTH economies)
- **Earned rewards skew toward prestige** — exclusive earned badges (can't be bought) + modest embers/boxes — NOT floods of embers, which would undercut the paid economy. The biggest earned payouts (mythic box for uni #1) are **rare by nature** (one person per season), so they don't flood.
- **Paid = volume + convenience + paid-exclusive cosmetics + Forge Pass.** Free users earn *status*; paying buys *variety and speed*. Both stay healthy.

### Anti-cheese (Step 18)
Big rewards (legendary/mythic boxes, exclusive badges) **ride on verified effort** — the "ridiculous goal" payouts especially must be effort-verified (min durations / device metrics), or a farmed legendary cheapens the status for everyone. Rewards are cosmetic/intrinsic, so farming is self-defeating anyway — but the *social status* of a rare earned badge is only worth protecting if it can't be farmed.
