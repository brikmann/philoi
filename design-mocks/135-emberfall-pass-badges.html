Art direction for the two new badges (for the illustrator / image-gen)

Molten Seal — Tier 26–50. The exact same hex seal as Tier 0–25, but heated to molten: the metal glows from within, white-hot cracks split its surface as it begins to soften, and the rim burns orange-gold. The central flame is large and roaring, sparks rising. No cold silver anywhere — this reads as radiant, near-melting fire-metal. Palette: molten orange, white-hot core.

Phoenix Rising — Tier 51–75. The seal has melted into a pool of molten metal, and from that pool a phoenix of pure fire rises — body glowing white-gold, wings of flame unfurling upward, only half-emerged (its lower form still molten metal below). This is the literal birth moment that completes in the Tier 76–100 Volcanic Crest, where the phoenix is fully risen. Palette: white-gold body, molten base, radiant heat.

Progression: cold seal → molten seal → phoenix rising from the molten metal → phoenix fully risen. The metal melts and becomes the bird.
