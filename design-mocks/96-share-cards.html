Mock 96 v2 — share cards (the retention loop)

Five story cards, one per component. Every card shows the sharer's rank in a hex + philoi.app in the footer, so each share is an install prompt with a status stamp.

The loop: earn a moment → tap Share → post to story → friends see the flex + rank + philoi.app → they install → they lock in → they hit their own moment.

Where Share lives (per card): fire = share icon next to the streak on Home · lock-in = Share on the done / daily-fire screen · rank-up = on the rank-up celebration · season = on the season-standing screen · unlock = on the unlock / box-open reveal. Each fires from the screen where the moment happens.
