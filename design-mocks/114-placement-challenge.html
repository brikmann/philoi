How placement fits

Scope scales the reward. A 6-person campfire placement is small; a 48-person class is bigger; uni-wide / season are the biggest — same percentile bands, hotter rewards as the pool grows.

Admin-run. The prof/admin sets it and starts it; students auto-enter by being in the course campfire (the "For a class?" toggle).

Effort, not grades. The metric is lock-in time (or volume/distance) — pure effort. Ties break on first-to-reach then most lock-in.

Reuses the engine. Percentile bands + rewards already exist (CHALLENGE_REWARD_ALGO / REWARD_ECONOMY) — this is that, scoped to a class over a custom window.

Where it sits. Same percentile engine as season/uni, scoped to a class/campfire over a custom span.
