How team mode works

Create (A): team mode is a campfire-only challenge type. Pick a sport from a big grid, name two teams (colour-coded), appoint a ref (the creator or any member). Reward is set by tier — winners a rarity up from losers. No individual metrics.
Campfire (B): the match posts as a live card in chat — sport, both teams' scores, a LIVE dot, who's reffing. Everyone watches it tick.
Ref view (C): a dedicated referee screen — big score per team, + / − controls (labelled to the sport: "+1 goal", or +1/+2/+3 for basketball), a match clock, Undo, and End match. Only the ref can edit; every change pushes live to the campfire card. This is the exclusive team/ref lock-in type — you're editing points between two teams, not logging a personal session.
Result (D): on End match, the higher score wins → the whole winning team gets the winner tier, the whole losing team gets the loser tier. Everyone who played is rewarded for showing up.
