The shared 3-beat pattern — standard tier crossing

1 · RISE (0–0.4s)Hex badge rises from the campfire flames at screen bottom, overshoots, settles. Tier-colored wash blooms.
2 · FLARE (0.4–0.9s)Metallic sweep crosses the badge + the tier's TIER_FLASH_KIND particles burst. Medium+success haptic, rank-up SFX.
3 · SETTLE (0.9–3s)Particles fade, badge locks, the all-caps copy fades up. Share offered. Division bumps = this pattern, lighter, NO copy.
