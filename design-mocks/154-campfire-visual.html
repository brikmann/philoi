Mock 154 — How a campfire looks

A campfire has two visual parts: the emoji the creator picks (its identity) sitting in a warm badge, and a flame that shows how alive it is right now (roaring · steady · cold). The emoji is theirs; the flame is the app's living signal. Consistent everywhere it appears.

So the emoji isn't ad-hoc — it's the campfire's chosen mark (from group.emoji), and we always frame it in the warm badge with the living flame: big and bright when the fire's roaring, small when steady, grey when it's gone cold. Same badge in every context — home valley, lists, invites, search, and the campfire header — so campfires read as campfires, not loose emojis.
