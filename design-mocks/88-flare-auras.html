The perimeter aura is the flare — a premium unlock, not a free-tier effect. When a flare is equipped it renders a faint glowing perimeter on every page in the app (shown here on a generic screen), scoped to that flare's colour + signature effect. Faint enough that the app stays fully usable; distinct enough to flex. No flare equipped → no aura.
