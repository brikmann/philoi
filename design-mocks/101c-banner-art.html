Bespoke banner art — one coded scene per banner

Each banner is its own animated scene. Emberfall tiers differ by a flashing Cindy flame. Ashfall = a cloaked torch-bearer raining ash. Great Forge = a smith striking a lit anvil in the black. Colosseum = the Roman arena, windows flickering. Night = constellations. Ashfall Ridge = ash over a distant city. Hearthlight keeps its quiet ridge.
