Easing the goal shrinks the reward to match (Legendary → Epic), so no one games an easy finish. A harder change needs a supermajority the owner sets (50–100%, e.g. 80%) — hit it and it auto-applies; miss it by the deadline and it fails. Outvoted members can leave with progress kept; owner/co-admin can override.
