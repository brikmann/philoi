Two marks, two jobs. The clean FlameLogo silhouette stays the brand (tab bar, app icon, wordmark, home hero at rest). This coal-bed campfire is the activity gauge. One heat ∈ [0,1] picks the composition: ≥0.6 a live cluster of staggered flame tongues (Inferno-flare licks) off a bright coal bed + sparks · 0.15–0.6 a few low, gentle tongues off a glowing ember bed · <0.15 dead grey coals + ash + several rising smoke puffs, no glow. The coal base is constant across all three — only the fire on top changes. Personal heat = streak + today's progress. Campfire heat = get_my_campfire_heat().
