The #1 feature real users asked for — but a warm nudge, not a wall. User-picked apps · auto every lock-in · soft pass-through. Native intercept (iOS Screen Time / Android) — see APP_BLOCKER_SPEC.md.

How it behaves: lock in → open a picked app and Philoi greets you with an AI-written message + real affordances. You can continue with no penalty — empathy, not force. ✨ Sonnet writes it from your data — sessions, goals, streak, burnout signals, and (if connected) your Google Calendar (real exams/deadlines/free-busy) — so lines like "midterm's Friday" are true, not guessed. It runs both directions: pull you back when it matters, bless a real break when you're fried, and re-engage when you've rested (Frame 4). 🛟 Safety-first: it never shames — repeatedly retreating to socials reads as avoidance, not laziness, so it leans to connection + restraint (go outside, text someone you trust) and, on real distress signals, points to support. Safety always beats "get back to work." GCal is the wedge that makes it a grade-improver, not a toy.
