Trade-off: One nav is cleaner and gives home more room, but Home/Leaderboards/Challenges/Profile now take two taps (open drawer → pick) instead of one — and those are the screens people hit most. Two navs keeps the core loop one tap away at the cost of a bottom bar. For an accountability app where people jump between Home ↔ Challenges ↔ Leaderboard constantly, one-tap access usually wins — but the single drawer is the more minimal look.
