Delete your Philoi account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Philoi account and the data associated with it, what is removed, and what we may keep and for how long. Philoi is developed by Philoi (contact: support@philoi.app).

How to delete your account

Fastest · in the app

Delete it yourself, in Settings

  1. Open the Philoi app and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation word when prompted, then tap Permanently delete my account.

Your account and its associated data are then deleted as described below. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

By email

Ask us to delete it for you

Email support@philoi.app from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account.” We’ll verify it’s you and process the deletion. We aim to complete email requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data tied to it, including:

  • Your profile — name, username, bio, school, and campus-verification email
  • Your email address and account identifiers
  • Your lock-in / study & fitness history, goals, streaks, and stats
  • Your health & fitness inputs (e.g. height and weight you entered, and fitness data synced from connected sources such as Health Connect / Apple Health / Strava)
  • Your photos, campfire messages, and posts
  • Your cosmetics, inventory, and in-app currency balance
  • Your notification/push tokens and in-app preferences

What we keep, and for how long

A limited amount of data may be retained after deletion where we’re legally required to, or to protect our service. Where retained, it is kept only as long as necessary and then permanently deleted.

DataWhy it’s keptRetention
Purchase / transaction recordsLegal, tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations (these are also held independently by Google Play and our payments processor)As required by law
BackupsDeleted data may persist in encrypted backups until they are cycled out Up to 90 days
Aggregated / anonymized analyticsNo longer identifies you; used to understand product usage Indefinite (anonymized)
Safety & moderation recordsWhere an account was actioned for a safety or policy violation, limited records may be kept to enforce our policies and meet legal dutiesAs required
