The climb

Rise through the pantheon.

Ten tiers, thirty divisions. Everyone starts in Bronze. Every hour you lock in forges you up the ladder — and past Diamond you leave the mortal climb behind. Almost nobody reaches Primordial. That is the point.

Your circle, around one fire.

For clubs & teams

Run your club on Philoi.

Intramural teams, varsity clubs, study societies, fraternities and sororities — anywhere attendance is the whole problem. Philoi gives you a private space, a season-long structure, and a leaderboard your members actually open.

  • A private campfire for the roster

    Invite-only. Members see each other train in real time — nobody else does.

  • Season-long training challenges

    Set a target for the term — sessions, hours, distance, volume — and let it score itself.

  • Team mode for game days

    One scorekeeper reports the final. Both rosters get credited and rewarded off it — no spreadsheet.

  • A leaderboard people check

    Ranks, streaks and standings your exec can point at in the group chat on a Sunday night.
