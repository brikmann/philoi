The algorithm

The only algorithm here is built to bring people together.

Every other app's algorithm is tuned for time-on-app. It pulls you deeper into things you don't even want: outrage, autoplay, the infinite scroll. Philoi's is tuned for one thing, connection. It reads shared interests, program, campus and who's around to surface people and rooms. Then it wants you to close the app and go meet them.

Competition

Yes, there's a leaderboard.
It's opt-in, and it's for fun.

Standings are a feature, not the point. They're off by default. What Philoi leads with is you against last week's you. The reason Philoi works is the integration, the matching and your community.

Competition is a switch you flip

Want a race with your friends? Turn on Standings. If you don't, you'll never see a ranking.

The name

φίλοι

Philoi · FEE-loy · Greek for “friends”

In the Hellenistic kingdoms, the Philoi were also a title of honour: the King’s Friends, the trusted inner circle a ruler named as his allies. To be named one meant, in effect, “you are my friend, I trust you, and we do good things for each other.”

Not followers. Not an audience. Allies.

That’s the promise. Philoi isn’t a feed you consume. It’s the circle you build: people you trust, who show up for you, and you for them. Your patron and your crest, your verified campus circle, the people who cheer your wins instead of ranking them all come back to this one word.

Your people are on campus.
Come find them.
