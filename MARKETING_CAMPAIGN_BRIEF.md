## 1. What I'm thinking (the idea)

**Philoi** is a social-accountability app for **Wilfrid Laurier + University of Waterloo** students — you and
your friends "lock in" together (study, gym, run, deep work), keep streaks, run challenges, and grow a shared
flame. It's Duolingo-style momentum meets a group chat that actually makes you show up.

**This campaign has one job: awareness.** It is **pre-launch hype** — we are *not* measuring whether people
love the app yet, only whether the right students **notice it, feel the vibe, and join the waitlist.** No
install or revenue targets. Build an audience the launch can land on.

**Why now:** fall term at Laurier/UW starts early September — students are re-forming study/gym routines and
their feeds are peak-active. This window is the single best awareness moment of the semester.

**Audience:** Laurier + UW undergrads, ~18–22. Study culture, gym culture, "lock in" energy, productivity,
friendly competition. Speak *their* language, on *their* campuses.

**The one message:** *"Lock in with your friends. Philoi is coming."* Everything ladders up to that.

**Core features to showcase (in priority order):**
1. **Campfires** — group accountability circles with your friends / your class.
2. **Lock-ins** — start a focus/gym/study session; your friends see you show up.
3. **Challenges** — head-to-head + group races to out-work each other.
4. **Streaks, ranks + the flame** — grow and customize your fire; climb the ladder.

## 2. Channels + content pillars

**Primary:** Instagram (Reels) + TikTok — the student awareness engines. Short-form video first.
**Support:** IG Stories (daily), and organic seeding into Laurier/UW communities (meme pages, r/wlu,
r/uwaterloo, class/residence Discords).

**Content pillars (rotate):**
- **Relatable student life** — the "I need to lock in but my friends distract me" tension Philoi solves.
- **The aesthetic** — the flame/ember look, streaks, ranks, cosmetics (screen-recordings of the app).
- **Friendly competition** — challenge/leaderboard moments ("who studies more this week").
- **Countdown / FOMO** — "coming this fall," waitlist call-to-action, launch countdown.

## 3. Execution checklist (how I want you to run it)

### A. Setup (week 1, first 3 days)
- [ ] Create/optimize **@philoi** on Instagram + TikTok — handle, profile photo (the flame), bio, **waitlist
      link in bio** (philoi.app).
- [ ] Lock the visual identity: flame/ember palette, consistent cover style, on-brand captions/voice.
- [ ] Build a **2.5-week content calendar** (dates, hooks, format, CTA) — share it with me before posting.
- [ ] Set up a simple metrics tracker (followers, reach, saves/shares, link clicks, waitlist signups) updated
      weekly.

### B. Content (ongoing, all weeks)
- [ ] Ship **4–5 short-form videos/week** (Reels + TikTok, cross-posted) — mix of POV/talking-head,
      app screen-recordings, and meme-style.
- [ ] Post **daily Stories** (polls, countdowns, behind-the-scenes, reposts).
- [ ] Every post has a clear CTA: **follow + join the waitlist.**
- [ ] Keep a batch of 3–4 posts queued ahead so we never go dark.

### C. Community + seeding (ongoing)
- [ ] Engage daily with Laurier/UW student accounts, meme pages, and hashtags (comment, follow, DM).
- [ ] Seed the app into **r/wlu, r/uwaterloo**, and relevant campus Discords/group chats (respect each
      community's rules — no spam).
- [ ] Identify **5–10 Laurier/UW student micro-creators** and pitch simple shoutouts/collabs (list them for my
      approval before outreach).

### D. Waitlist funnel
- [ ] Drive every channel to the **waitlist** (link in bio + Story links); report signups weekly.

### E. Reporting
- [ ] **Weekly check-in (15 min)** + a short written update: what shipped, the numbers, what's working, next
      week's plan.
- [ ] **End-of-campaign wrap** (by ~Sept 12): total reach/followers/waitlist, top-performing posts, and a
