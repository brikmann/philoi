# Philoi — Go-to-Market Plan (Aug–Sept 2026)

Two channels, committed: **gym table (primary)** + **lecture pitches (awareness)**, with **word of
mouth** as the connective tissue. Solo student founder, low budget, works around a class schedule.
Grassroots download = no Laurier IT vetting (students choose to install it). Gym tabling needs only a
facilities permission.

## Positioning
- **One-liner:** "Philoi turns studying and working out into a game you play with your friends — lock
  in, climb the leaderboard, don't break the streak."
- **10-second gym pitch:** "It's a free app I built for students — start a timer when you study or work
  out, and compete with your friends and the rest of campus on streaks and leaderboards. Laurier vs
  Waterloo, basically. Want the link?"
- **Founder hook:** "I'm a third-year Kin student here — I built this myself." People root for a student
  founder; it's your credibility *and* your relatability.

## Channel 1 — Gym table (PRIMARY, your ICP)
Your target user literally walks past you, twice a day (in + out). Gym-goers already track activity;
Philoi rewards exactly what they're already doing.

**Poster / QR brief**
- Headline (readable from 3m): **"STUDY + GYM — BUT MAKE IT A GAME"** (or "LOCK IN. CLIMB. FLEX.")
- Sub: "A free app for Laurier students. Compete with your friends and the rest of campus."
- Visual: twilight / Greek-fire aesthetic; a phone showing the flame + a leaderboard.
- **QR front-and-center** → your download smart link (TestFlight now, App Store later).
- Footer: "Built by a Laurier Kin student → [arrow to you]. Ask me anything."

**Tabling logistics**
- Spot: the choke point by the athletic-complex front desk / entry — everyone passes it.
- Windows: your 2–3 highest-traffic slots that fit class (typically ~11am–2pm and ~4–7pm). Do remote
  schoolwork between conversations — that's the whole low-effort appeal.
- Bring: standing foam-board poster, a QR tent card, laptop (work + live demo), a phone with the app
  open, and a cheap incentive if budget allows (stickers / a small draw for signups).

**Opening line (low-pressure):** "Hey — you here a lot? I built a free app for Laurier students, takes
10 seconds to show you." → 10-sec pitch → "scan this or I can text you the link."

**Convert on the spot:** get them to scan + install *while standing there*, then "start your first
lock-in right now, 20 seconds." First-session activation is the retention hook — don't let them walk
away with just a download.

**"What is it / why care":** anchor to what they're doing — "you already work out; this makes it count —
streaks, leaderboards, cosmetics. And your study time counts too."

**Laurier gym permission — draft message**
> Hi [Athletic Complex / facilities team], I'm Noah Brikman, a third-year Kinesiology student. I've
> built a free wellness/productivity app for Laurier students and I'd love to set up a small table near
> the entrance for a few hours on [days] to demo it and answer questions. Nothing's being sold — it's a
> founder-led student demo. Happy to follow any tabling guidelines you have. Could we set that up?

**UW rotation + hire-a-rep**
- Rotate to the UW PAC/CIF gym on off days (same setup: "built by a Laurier student, expanding to
  Waterloo").
- Or hire ONE rep to staff the other campus so you cover both at once. Pay ~$18–22/hr, or a per-signup
  bonus (~$1/verified signup, capped). Brief them with a one-pager: the 10-sec pitch, the QR, top-5
  FAQ answers, and a fallback — "the founder's a Laurier student, here's his handle."

## Channel 2 — Lecture pitches (AWARENESS — a few hundred students fast)
**The ask to a prof:** "Hi Professor — I'm a Laurier student and I built an app for students. Could I
have 2 minutes at the *start* of a lecture in the first week to show the class? Fully optional for
them." Keep it an **announcement, not integration** — that's what avoids IT vetting.

**Prioritize:** big **first-year** lectures in the **first two weeks of September** (Kin KP100-level;
Business BU111/BU121 — the profs already in your tracker). First-years are highest-LTV: four years
ahead of them, forming habits and friend groups right now.

**2-minute script**
> "Hey everyone — I'm Noah, third-year Kin. I built Philoi, a free app that turns studying and working
> out into a game you play with friends. You start a timer when you lock in, build streaks, and climb
> leaderboards — your class, all of Laurier, even Laurier vs Waterloo. First week of the semester is
> when habits actually stick, so this is the time. Scan the code — 10 seconds. Thanks, Professor [name]."

Keep a **full-screen QR slide** up the whole time: "Philoi — free — scan to join."

## Channel 3 — Word of mouth / referral
- The app already has friends/campfires — make the invite dead simple + rewarding (invite a friend →
  both get embers / a cosmetic).
- Lean into **Laurier-vs-Waterloo** leaderboards as shareable bragging rights (the share cards exist).
- **Seed first:** get your own network on it (Kin cohort, res friends, StartUp Lab) BEFORE strangers
  arrive, so the leaderboards aren't empty on day one.

## Cansbridge (Sept 5)
Don't hard-pitch a room of high-agency people — **demo it, get them on it, ask for brutal feedback, and
collect intros** (founders at other schools who could run a gym table at THEIR campus). Frame Philoi as
"the accountability layer for people who lock in" — that room self-selects for exactly that.

## Week-by-week calendar
- **Aug 14–20:** finish v1 + submit to App Store. TestFlight live → recruit 20–30 friends as first
  users (seed leaderboards). Design + print the gym poster. Message Laurier gym for tabling permission.
- **Aug 21–31:** App Store approval; soft-launch to your network. Line up UW gym permission + a rep if
  hiring. Confirm first-week lecture slots with the profs in the tracker.
- **Sept 1–5:** Cansbridge (Sept 5) — demo + collect founder intros. Lock the gym-table schedule for the
  first weeks of term.
- **Sept 8–19 (first 2 weeks of classes — PEAK):** gym table 3–4 windows/week at Laurier (+ UW via
  rep/rotation); lecture pitches in the big first-year Kin/business courses; push the referral loop.
  This is THE acquisition window — go hard.
- **Sept 20+:** measure, double down on the channel that converts best, keep the gym table as the steady
  drip.

## Metrics (weekly)
- **Downloads by channel** — use a distinct QR/link per channel (gym vs lecture vs Cansbridge) so you
  know what's working.
- **Activation** = % who complete a first lock-in within 24h (the real signal, not just installs).
- **D1 / D7 retention.**
- **Gym-table conversion** = downloads ÷ conversations.
- **Lecture conversion** = downloads ÷ class size.
- **Referral** = invites sent → accepted.
