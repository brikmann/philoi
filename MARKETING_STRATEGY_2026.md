# Philoi — marketing strategy (pre-launch → rest of 2026)

**Guiding principle:** no marketing on vapor. Build V1 in full first; market a product that's actually ready.
Content is produced *during* the build so it can fire on a launch date **we control** — not on Apple's review
queue.

---

## Phase 0 — during the build (now)
- **No public marketing.** V1 features still being scoped; still building. Marketing an unfinished/unproven
  app burns first impressions in the exact campus communities we can't re-acquire.
- **Produce the campaign content now, in parallel:** the burst videos take real time and some need the working
  app to screen-record. Have everything ready to fire on command.
- Set up the accounts + the #LightYourFire kit; seed the hashtag privately (Noah + friends posting real
  lock-ins) so it isn't an empty tag on day one.
- 🔴 **Gate:** the core loop (challenges settling, campfires, streaks) must be verified live before any push.
  The campaign is top-of-funnel; the product is the retention. A leaky loop makes the whole funnel wasted.

## Phase 1 — the launch burst (#LightYourFire)
Coordinated online awareness bursts, fired at a **controlled launch date** (approved build / set day — NOT "App
Store pending," which is unpredictable and rejection-prone).
- **Campaign:** #LightYourFire — students show how they *light their fire*: studying, gym, group challenges,
  flexing progress. Make it a **UGC behavior**, not just branded promo.
- **Content:**
  - **1 polished hero film** (Seedance 2.0 — feed it real app screens + flame brand as references so it's
    on-brand, not hallucinated; cinematic + native audio for the vibe).
  - **Feature deep-dives** (campfires · leaderboards · cosmetics · gym progress) — use **real screen-recordings**
    of the actual UI so expectations match reality; AI only for atmosphere.
  - **Volume of scrappy, native, real-person POV** content — this is what actually spreads on student TikTok.
    Over-glossy AI reels get views, not belief.
- **Channels:**
  - **IG + TikTok** — primary, general student base.
  - **LinkedIn** — Noah's founder / build-in-public story (credibility, press, investors) — NOT user
    acquisition.
  - **Reddit / Discord** — 🔴 authentic, value-first, founder-led participation ONLY. Never drop the campaign
    here; r/wlu, r/uwaterloo + campus Discords treat that as astroturf → bans + backlash in the core community.
- **KPIs:** views, likes, reach, saves/shares (awareness). Maybe a waitlist. **Not** a conversion campaign —
  there's nothing to convert; the job is spread.

## Phase 2 — on campus (the backbone)
- The moment Noah is on campus: **in-person seeding at Laurier** — posters + cluster seeding (a res floor, a
  club, a study group, an intramural team on it *together*), physical QR hooks ("lock in with your floor").
  A social app dies solo; seed **friend-clusters**, not individuals.
- This is the go-forward WOM engine, weighted Laurier + UW (same city, verified core).

## Phase 3 — hire, if it's working (Acadium)
- **Trigger (pre-committed):** if, ~2 weeks post-launch, there's real traction — active campfires forming and
  **inviting more members** (cluster/invite loops), decent D1/D7 at Laurier — then hire.
- **The hire:** one Acadium intern, **$840 + tax / quarter, ~10 hrs/week, 3 months** → CMO for the
  Laurier–Waterloo region, owning growth across social + in-person. Option to hire directly after the
  internship (not under Acadium contract). One focused person > four unmanaged juniors while in full-time school.

---

## KPIs by phase
| Phase | What we measure |
|---|---|
| Launch burst | views · likes · reach · shares (awareness). Maybe waitlist. |
| On campus | **active campfires** · first-lock-in completion · D1/D7 retention · invites sent per user |
| Post-hire | cluster growth per campus · retention curve · CAC-per-channel (in-person vs social) |

## Guardrails
- Loop must be verified live before any volume push (top-of-funnel is wasted on a broken product).
- Reddit/Discord: participate authentically, never campaign-drop.
- AI video stays on-brand (real references) and honest (real UI for features).
- Seed #LightYourFire with real UGC before launch day.
- Awareness ≠ retention: the campaign fills the top; the product holds the bottom.
