# Philoi Campus Seeding Playbook — Laurier + Waterloo
*Your in-person marketing plan for the semester. Goal: light the fire on two neighbouring campuses, across demographics, so the inter-uni rivalry engine self-sustains.*

> **Note:** club rosters change every year — the specific clubs below are real/known, but confirm they're active and grab current exec contacts from the live directories: **[clubs.wusa.ca](https://clubs.wusa.ca/club_listings)** (Waterloo), **[yourstudentsunion.ca/club-listings](https://www.yourstudentsunion.ca/club-listings)** and **[Laurier sanctioned sport clubs](https://recreation.laurierathletics.com/)** (Laurier).

---

## 1 · The thesis (why this works)
- **Seed pods, not people.** Philoi is social — one person in an empty campfire bounces; 5 friends who join together stick. Every target below is a *pre-built pod* (a floor, a team, a club exec + members).
- **Density per campus, not national awareness.** Get *one campus* culturally hot before the next — Facebook did Harvard, then Columbia. Your metric is per-campus density, not total signups.
- **Rivalry is the growth engine.** Laurier and Waterloo are ~1 km apart with a real little-brother/big-brother rivalry. Once Laurier is hot and "winning" the inter-uni board, that *provokes* Waterloo to download defensively. Seeding Laurier well IS how you prime Waterloo.
- **First impression = permanent identity.** Adopt-for-yourself/your-friends first; the class-tool and uni-competition are layers on top, never the entry. Spread across demographics early so Philoi reads as "the thing everyone uses," not "the gym app" or "the class app."

## 2 · The seeding method (how to run every contact)
- **Framing:** you're offering a *free accountability + community tool their group can run together* — a private campfire, a group challenge — NOT "please let me advertise." Partnership/pilot, never solicitation.
- **The ask (small, concrete):** "Can I set your exec/team up with a private Philoi campfire and run a 1–2 week challenge?" A challenge with a start date is the hook — it's a reason for the whole pod to join at once.
- **The offer:** free; a private campfire for the group; cosmetics/embers for participants; bragging rights on a leaderboard (and, later, on the inter-uni board). Cheap to give, high perceived value.
- **Do:** land the whole exec + a chunk of members together; pick a start date; give them a reason to invite *their* people (that's the loop).
- **Don't:** cold-postal residences without Res Life sign-off (controlled space, can get you banned); let a professor make it mandatory for a grade (poisons the anchor); pitch it as single-vertical ("it's for the gym").
- **Lead with trust** wherever first-years/minors are involved (residences, orientation): mention data handling + child-safety + HECVAT-in-progress. It's the difference between a yes and a no.

## 3 · Clearances / who the gatekeeper actually is
| Channel | Who says yes | Friction | How to approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clubs (both campuses)** | The **club president/exec directly** | **Lowest** — clubs choose their own tools | DM/email the exec; offer a private campfire + challenge. Start here. |
| **Residences** | **Manager/Director of Residence Life** (Res Life, under Student Affairs) — *not* individual dons | Medium-high (protective of first-years) | Pitch a **pilot** ("a floor lock-in challenge with Res Life"), not an ad. Dons are the channel; Res Life is the gate. Confirm the exact title before reaching out. |
| **Athletics / sport clubs / intramurals** | **Department of Recreation / Athletics** + individual sport-club execs | Low–medium | Sport-club execs directly for a challenge; Rec dept for anything intramural-wide. |
| **Orientation / O-Week** | Orientation office + **icebreakers/leaders** | Medium (seasonal) | Best in Sept; leaders are perfect carriers into first-year pods. |
| **Anything "official" / union-wide** | **WUSA** (Waterloo) / **Laurier Students' Union** | Higher | Only once you have traction — don't lead here. |

## 6 · Sequencing + activating the rivalry
1. **Phase 1 — Laurier residence beachhead.** Willison + 1–2 dorms via a Res Life pilot. Get floors as campfires. *Goal: local density where you have the most access.*
2. **Phase 2 — Laurier clubs, ONE per demographic.** Deliberately spread: one athletic (a sport club), one business (SBESS), one tech (GDSC), one wellness. This makes Philoi read as *cross-domain* from day one — not "the gym app." *Goal: identity = "everyone here uses it."*
3. **Phase 3 — Waterloo beachhead pod.** Drop a small friend-group / one affiliate college (Renison or Grebel) + one marquee design team. You only need a spark — the rivalry does the rest.
4. **Phase 4 — activate the Laurier-vs-Waterloo board.** Once Laurier is visibly "winning," surface it: that provokes Waterloo to defend. Now Waterloo clubs/residences pull *themselves* in. The board self-sustains.

**Density trigger (know when to flip Phase 4):** define it as a number, not a vibe — e.g. *N active campfires on a campus, X% of a target residence/club active, organic (unprompted) campfire-creation happening without you.* When Laurier hits it, make the rivalry loud. Until then, keep seeding quietly.

## 7 · The one rule that ties it all together
Every contact, ask: **does this first touch feel like an obligation or an identity?** Optimize relentlessly for identity — a club/floor *choosing* to run a challenge together (identity) beats a prof/admin *assigning* it (obligation), even when the assignment would get more raw signups. The rivalry and the class-tool are layers you add *after* the identity is formed, never the entry.

## 8 · Outreach scripts (adapt the voice)

**Club exec (DM/email) — lowest friction, start here:**
> Hey [name] — I built Philoi, an app that turns studying/training/goals into a shared streak game with your friends (think Strava meets a campfire). I'd love to set [club] up with a private campfire and run a 1–2 week challenge for your members — free, and I'll throw in some in-app rewards for everyone who joins. Could I grab 10 min with you or the exec this week?

**Sport club / rec:**
> Hey [name] — Philoi lets a team run a training-accountability challenge together (log sessions, climb a leaderboard, earn rewards). Want to run one for [club] this month? I'll set up your private campfire and get everyone in on day one.

**Residence Life (formal — pilot framing):**
> Hi [name] — I'm a Laurier student and I built Philoi, a student accountability + community app. I'd love to pilot a small "residence lock-in challenge" with one floor/building this term — opt-in, run with your dons, designed around community and healthy habits. Happy to share how we handle student data + safety (we're completing the higher-ed HECVAT). Could we find 15 min?

**Don (individual champion — informal):**
> Hey [name] — I made an app that runs floor-vs-floor accountability challenges. If Res Life's good with it, would you be up to run one on your floor this term? I'll set it all up — you just rally your people.
